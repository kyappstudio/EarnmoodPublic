隱私政策

版本 1.0

一句話

外送薪情沒有帳號、沒有自己的伺服器,也沒有任何分析或崩潰回報工具。你記的班次、趟次、成本、結算與設定都留在自己的裝置上,不會被傳出去。

唯一會連上網路的是升級 Pro 相關的兩件事:取得方案價格、確認你買過什麼。詳見〈付費〉與〈網路權限〉兩節。

這個 App 沒有廣告,也不會有。

我們不做什麼

不要求註冊或登入,沒有帳號可以建立,也沒有地方讓你填 Email。

不讀取位置、通訊錄、通話、簡訊、麥克風,沒有相機權限,不使用無障礙服務,也不讀取其他 App 的通知。

不會自動抓取外送平台的收入資料。每一筆班次、單數與金額都是你自己輸入的。

沒有內建分析工具、沒有崩潰回報,也沒有第三方 SDK 在背景記錄你怎麼使用這個 App。

法規與平台資料

App 內建的專法最低報酬公式、平台結算週期與行情帶,是隨安裝包提供的資料檔,並標示資料日期。核對結果全部是參考值,不會依你的資料向任何地方查詢,也不構成法律意見;實際補發以平台的核對為準。

你的資料存在哪裡

班次、趟次核對紀錄、成本、結算與入帳狀態、平台設定與所有偏好,都存在 App 自己的本機儲存空間裡,只有這個 App 讀得到。移除 App 就會一併刪除。

App 已關閉 Android 系統的「自動備份」,所以系統不會把 App 的資料複製到你的 Google 雲端硬碟。要保留一份副本,請用 App 內的「匯出備份」。

通知權限

用來在入帳日與補發核對日提醒你對帳。

所有通知都是在你的裝置上排程與顯示的本機通知,不經過任何推播服務,也沒有任何內容離開裝置。

不給這個權限只會讓提醒不出現,其他功能照常。

精確鬧鐘與開機權限

App 使用 Android 的精確鬧鐘,在你設定的時間發出提醒;若你不允許精確鬧鐘,提醒會改在前後 15 分鐘的視窗內送出。開機完成的權限用來在手機重開後把提醒重新排好。兩者都只在裝置上運作。

網路權限

App 會在兩個時候連上網路:你打開外送薪情 Pro 的畫面時,向 Google Play 取得方案的價格;以及每次啟動時,向 Google Play 確認你是否買過 Pro。這兩件事都不會送出你記的任何資料。

其他功能 —— 班次與 +1、真實時薪、專法核對、結算日曆、提醒、統計與匯出、備份匯出與匯入、桌面 Widget —— 全部在裝置上完成。

備份匯出與匯入

備份檔是在你按下「匯出備份」的當下,於裝置上即時產生的 zip 檔,裡面有你的全部資料。存到哪裡由你在系統的檔案選擇器裡決定,我們不會上傳,也不知道你放在哪裡或分享給誰。

匯入時只讀取你選的那一個檔案,一樣不經過網路。CSV 匯出也是同樣的方式。

桌面 Widget

Widget 顯示的段位、時薪與進行中的班次全部來自裝置上的 App 資料,不會為了 Widget 連線。

兒童

這個 App 不是為兒童設計的,對象是外送員。我們不蒐集任何人的個人資料,兒童也不例外。

付費

外送薪情 Pro 分成一次買斷與年費兩種,交易一律由 Google Play 處理。App 只會收到「你是不是 Pro」這一項結果,不會接觸你的付款資訊,也沒有使用其他第三方付費服務。

App 會把「是 Pro」的結果記在裝置上,離線時最多沿用 7 天,之後再向 Google Play 確認一次。

年費會依週期自動續訂,直到你取消為止。取消要到 Google Play 的訂閱設定裡操作,取消後仍可使用到當期結束。退款依 Google Play 的政策辦理。

政策的變更

政策若有修改,會更新下面的版本,並隨 App 更新一起發布。

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

In short

Earnmood has no accounts, no server of our own, and no analytics or crash reporting of any kind. Your shifts, orders, costs, payouts and settings stay on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The only thing that goes online is Pro itself: fetching plan prices and checking what you have purchased. See the Payments and Internet permission sections.

There are no ads in this app, and there will not be.

What we do not do

No sign-up, no login, no account to create, and nowhere to enter an email address.

No access to location, contacts, phone, SMS or microphone, no camera permission, no accessibility service, and the app never reads other apps' notifications.

The app never captures income from delivery platforms automatically. Every shift, order count and amount is typed in by you.

No analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDK recording how you use the app.

Legal and platform data

The minimum-pay formula, platform settlement calendars and market bands are a data file shipped with the app, labelled with its data date. Every check result is a reference value; nothing about your data is looked up anywhere, and none of it is legal advice. The platform's own review decides what is actually paid.

Where your data lives

Shifts, checked orders, costs, payouts and their status, platform settings and every preference are held in the app's own local storage, readable only by this app. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app opts out of Android Auto Backup, so the system will not copy its data to your Google Drive. To keep a copy, use "Export backup" inside the app.

Notification permission

Used to remind you on payday and on true-up days to check what arrived.

Every notification is scheduled and shown locally on your device. No push service is involved and nothing leaves the device.

Declining it only means reminders will not appear; everything else works as usual.

Exact alarm and boot permissions

The app uses Android's exact alarms to remind you at the time you chose; if you decline exact alarms, reminders arrive within a 15-minute window instead. The boot-completed permission re-schedules reminders after a restart. Both work entirely on the device.

Internet permission

The app goes online in two situations: when you open the Earnmood Pro screen, to fetch plan prices from Google Play; and at each launch, to check with Google Play whether you have purchased Pro. Neither sends any of the data you have recorded.

Everything else — shifts and +1, true hourly, the legal minimum check, the settlement calendar, reminders, stats and export, backup export and import, the home-screen widget — happens on your device.

Backup export and import

The backup is a zip file generated on your device the moment you tap "Export backup". It contains all your data. Where it goes is decided by you in the system file picker; we never upload it and have no idea where you keep it or who you share it with.

Importing reads only the single file you pick, again without any network connection. CSV export works the same way.

Home-screen widget

Everything the widget shows — rank, hourly, the running shift — comes from the app's data on your device. It never connects to the network.

Children

This app is not designed for children; it is for delivery riders. We collect no personal data from anyone, children included.

Payments

Earnmood Pro comes as a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription. Transactions are handled by Google Play. The app receives only whether you are Pro, never your payment details, and no other payment service is involved.

The app remembers the Pro result on the device and keeps using it for up to 7 days while offline, then checks with Google Play again.

The yearly plan renews automatically until you cancel. Cancelling is done in your Google Play subscription settings, and access continues to the end of the current period. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the version below is updated and shipped with an app update.
